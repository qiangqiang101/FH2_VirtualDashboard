VirtualDashboard - Changelog

v1.1.0 (changes since v1.0.1)
-----------------------------
New
- Dashboard keeps working in all game menus, including the in-game Telemetry
  screen. It shows "Waiting for a car in the game..." only during loading,
  after a car swap, or when the connection to the PC is lost.
- Tap the gear to show the current speed in its place. The gear moves to the speedometer centre. Tap again to go back.
- RPM shows the digital value rounded to 50 by default (tap the gauge to switch).
- Raw telemetry list: brake, handbrake, turbo boost (bar), power (kW),
  torque (Nm), game time (HH:MM) and weather values.

Fixed
- The old "Brake" value was actually the clutch. It is now labelled Clutch,
  and the real brake and handbrake values are read.
- "Connection lost" now clears the displayed values instead of freezing them.

Known issues
- Power and torque are approximations (derived from an engine-power value) and
  can differ from the in-game Telemetry page.
- Turn signals are mirrored from the D-pad, not read from the game.
